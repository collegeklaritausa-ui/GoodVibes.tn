# MEGA PROMPT: The Resurrection of Success - Trilingual Master Script

**Project:** GoodVibes.tn Cinematic Marketing Film
**Runtime:** 5:00 (Target)
**Visuals:** High-contrast, cinematic. Transition from cold blue to warm gold.
**Audio:** Original score, rising in tempo and intensity. Protagonist speaks French, English, and Arabic.
**Objective:** Create an award-winning film foundation that drives traffic to the marketplace at [http://vendeur.site/](http://vendeur.site/).
